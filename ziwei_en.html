Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to this app (the “Service”). We take your personal data and privacy seriously. In line with Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and related regulations, this Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) explains how we collect, process, use and protect your personal data. Please read it carefully before using the Service. By using the Service, you confirm that you have read, understood and agreed to this Policy.

1. Principles

We follow Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and these principles:

  • Accountability and transparency: We explain why data is collected, how it is used, and who is responsible when you enter data about other people.
  • Data minimization: We only process what the Service needs. For other people, the only thing sent for AI analysis is the Si-Hua derived from their birth year; names, birth dates and notes are never sent.
  • Purpose limitation: Data is only used to provide chart readings and AI consultations.
  • Your authorization: If you enter birth details or other information about another person (family, friends, coworkers, partners), you must have their consent or other lawful authorization. You are responsible for any legal consequences of entering someone's data without it.
  • Security: We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, and keep your data on your own device.

2. How data is collected, used and stored

(1) No account or identity data

The Service has no sign-up or login, and never asks for your national ID number, phone number or email address.

(2) On-device storage

Your natal profile (birth date, time, gender, birthplace), people list (the nickname you choose, birth year, optional birth date and notes), AI reading history and settings are stored on your device. We do not upload them to our own servers. If iCloud or Google backup is turned on, this data may be included in your device backup and handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

(3) AI analysis via Google Gemini API

To provide chart readings and quick questions, the Service uses the Google Gemini API:

  • What is sent: Only the structured chart data needed for that request (palaces, stars, annual/monthly/daily transits, and each person's birth-year Si-Hua), plus the text of any quick question you type. People's names are never sent; they are replaced by codes such as T1 and T2. Anything you type into a question yourself is sent as written. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) through our relay server to the Gemini API.
  • Retention: The data is used only to generate that response. The Service keeps nothing after the response and never links the request to your identity. Please avoid typing names, ID numbers, bank details or other sensitive information into questions.
  • Google's terms: Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy and Google API Terms of Service.

(4) No analytics

The Service does not use any third-party analytics or behavior tracking tools.

(5) Advertising

The free version may show ads through a third-party ad service such as Google AdMob. Ad providers may collect your device's advertising ID, basic device information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, under their own privacy policies. Ads are removed once you upgrade to Pro.

(6) Payments

Pro purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details, only a receipt used to confirm your plan.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

The Service is a mobile app. It does not use cookies or track you across other apps.

Ad services may use your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA). You can reset it, limit ad personalization or opt out of tracking at any time in your device settings.

4. System permissions

The Service only asks for these permissions, with your consent, when you use the related feature:

  • Location (while using the app): When you tap “Current location” while entering a birthplace, the app reads your approximate coordinates once to fill in the longitude for a more accurate chart. Your location is used only on your device and is never sent to any server.
  • Biometrics (Face ID / fingerprint): Used to unlock the app when App Lock is on. Recognition is handled by the operating system; the Service never receives your biometric data.

5. Security

  • Protection: We use industry-standard measures such as SSL/TLS encryption and HTTPS to prevent unauthorized access, leaks, tampering or loss while data travels to the API or our servers.
  • Internet risk: No transmission over the internet is completely secure. Please do not send sensitive personal data over unencrypted channels.

6. Disclosure to third parties

Apart from the services named in this Policy (Google Gemini API, ad services, Apple App Store / Google Play), we never sell, rent, trade or disclose your personal data to third parties, except:

  • When required by law: At the request of courts, police or regulators under legal procedures.
  • To protect life and safety: To prevent imminent danger to the life, body, freedom or property of you or others.

7. Your rights

Under Article 3 of Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  1. Inquire about or review your data.
  2. Request a copy.
  3. Request additions or corrections.
  4. Request that collection, processing or use stop.
  5. Request deletion.

Note: Your chart and people data are stored entirely on your device. You can edit or delete them in the app at any time, or remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy for legal, operational, AI technology or service changes. Updates will be posted on our website or in the app with a new effective date. Continuing to use the Service after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
