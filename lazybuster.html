This Privacy Policy explains how LazyBuster handles information when you use the iOS app. We designed LazyBuster so that motion detection, custom sounds, and your challenge history stay on your device.

1. Summary

  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not use tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that send data to us.
  • Camera frames used for motion detection are processed on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Microphone access is used only if you record a custom alarm sound. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Challenge results, settings, and streaks are stored locally on your device.
  • If you contact us by email, we will receive whatever you include in that message.

2. Information we do not collect

LazyBuster does not collect or transmit:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or other contact details from inside the app
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Contacts, photos library, or calendar
  • Health, fitness, or financial information
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or tracking data
  • Analytics events, usage logs, or crash reports sent to our servers
  • Video or photo uploads of you or anyone in front of the camera

The app does not include in-app purchases, subscriptions, or third-party ads.

3. Information processed or stored on your device

The following stays on your iPhone or iPad unless you delete the app or its data.

Camera (motion detection)

During a Battle Zone challenge, LazyBuster uses the camera to tell whether you are moving or sitting still. The app takes small snapshots on your device, compares them locally, and discards them after the comparison. We do not save these snapshots to your photo library, and we do not upload them.

Camera access is requested so the challenge can run. You can deny or later revoke it in iOS Settings → LazyBuster → Camera. Without camera access, motion challenges cannot run.

Microphone (optional custom sounds)

The microphone is used only if you choose to record a custom alarm or fail sound in Settings. Built-in sounds work without the microphone.

Recordings are stored locally on your device for playback in the app. We do not upload them.

You can deny or later revoke microphone access in iOS Settings → LazyBuster → Microphone.

Imported audio files (optional)

If you import an audio file for a custom sound, that file is used locally in the app. We do not upload it.

Challenge history and settings

The app stores on your device:

  • Daily check-in results (success or fail, date, challenge mode, duration, alarm count)
  • Streaks and totals shown in Hall of Fame
  • App settings (selected mode, sound and haptics preferences, custom sound references)

This data is stored with the app (local device storage). It is not synced to iCloud by us, not backed up to our servers, and not linked to an account. Deleting the app, or clearing the app’s data, permanently removes this information.

4. How we use information

  • On-device camera processing is used only to detect motion during a challenge.
  • On-device audio is used only to play alarms and custom sounds you chose.
  • Local history is used only to show your check-ins, streaks, and Hall of Fame.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or selling data.

5. Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your information to third parties, because the app does not send your personal data to us or to other companies.

Email support: If you email us, we use your message only to reply and to fix the issue you reported. We do not use support email for marketing.

Service providers we do not use in this app: LazyBuster does not include third-party analytics, advertising, social, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Operating system and App Store: Apple may process information according to Apple’s own policies when you download the app from the App Store or use iOS features (for example, system permission prompts). That processing is controlled by Apple, not by LazyBuster.

6. Children’s privacy

LazyBuster is a family-friendly motion challenge app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Camera and microphone data are processed or stored only on the device, as described above, and are not sent to us. Parents and guardians should supervise use of the camera and any custom voice recordings.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact brianlin2021@gmail.com and we will delete that message.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Camera snapshots used for motion detection are not kept after they are compared on the device.
  • Custom sounds, settings, and challenge history remain on the device until you delete them by removing the app or clearing the app’s data.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Because we do not operate user accounts or a cloud database of your app data, we cannot remotely retrieve or restore your local history.

8. Your choices

  • Camera / Microphone: iOS Settings → LazyBuster.
  • Sound and haptics: in-app Settings.
  • Local history: delete the app (or offload/clear app data) to remove stored records and custom sounds.

On iOS you can also review tracking settings in Settings → Privacy & Security. LazyBuster does not request App Tracking Transparency permission because it does not track you across apps or websites.

9. International users

LazyBuster does not transfer your in-app data to our servers. If you email us, that message is received by us at the address you write to.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app’s behavior changes (for example, if we add cloud features). The “Last updated” date at the top will change, and the new policy will be posted at this URL. If we start collecting personal data, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent.
